# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Assume the person on the other end is acting in
good faith and knows things you do not.

## What we expect

- Discuss the work, not the person. Review code, not competence.
- Say what you mean plainly. Directness is welcome; contempt is not.
- Accept that people work in different languages, timezones and levels of
  context. This project is bilingual for a reason.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody keeps score here.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualised language,
deliberate intimidation, publishing others' private information, or sustained
disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ZYB10/branch-radar-oss). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that conflict
with this document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe behaviour.

Maintainers are held to this document too.
